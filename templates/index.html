ABOUT THE PROJECT

What is TRAFFIX?

TRAFFIX is an intelligent traffic monitoring and management concept designed to make roads smarter and urban transportation more efficient. It analyses traffic conditions, estimates congestion and supports adaptive traffic signal management.
